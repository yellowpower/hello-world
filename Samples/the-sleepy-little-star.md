# The Sleepy Little Star

High above the rooftops, where the sky turns deep and blue, there lived a little
star named Pip. Pip was the smallest star in the whole night sky, but Pip had
the warmest, gentlest light of all.

## A Busy Night

Every evening, when the sun yawned and slipped behind the hills, Pip woke up and
began to shine. One by one, the other stars blinked awake too, until the sky was
full of twinkling friends. The moon, round and silver, smiled down at them all.

"Good evening, little Pip," said the moon softly. "Are you ready to help the
children sleep?"

"I am!" said Pip, glowing a little brighter. For that was Pip's favorite job: to
watch over sleepy children and keep them safe until morning.

## Drifting Off

Down below, a child lay tucked beneath a cozy blanket, watching the stars
through the window. Pip floated closer and wrapped the room in soft, golden
light. The blanket felt warmer. The pillow felt softer.

"Close your eyes," whispered Pip, "and I will stay right here."

The child's breathing grew slow and calm, like waves rolling gently onto a quiet
shore. In and out. In and out. The room was peaceful and still.

And so, with the moon keeping watch and little Pip shining bright, the whole
world settled down to rest. Goodnight, stars. Goodnight, moon. Goodnight, sleepy
little one.

The end.
